## Vox style guide for the inserts

**Look**
- Paper background `#F3ECDC` with film grain and a soft vignette. Ink `#1B1B1B`, brand purple `#6A3FA0`, highlighter `#FFE14D`, annotation red `#E4472B`.
- Headlines in Playfair Display 900. Body text in Inter. Margin notes in Caveat (red, "handwritten").
- Footage never sits raw against the paper. Frame it as a polaroid or paper cutout with a drop shadow, or full-bleed with a paper lower third.

**Recurring device: the brick wall**
It appears in V2 (leaking), V3 (being scratched), V4 (bacteria on top) and V8 (rebuilt). Viewers track one object changing state, and that is what makes a Vox explainer feel coherent. Keep its position and size identical across scenes so cuts feel like the same diagram evolving.

**Transitions that make the footage and inserts flow**
1. **Zoom-through match cut** (Footage C → V2): scale into the elbow, cross-dissolve the skin texture into the drawn wall.
2. **Freeze and paper slide** (B → V1, D → V6): freeze the last frame, desaturate 50 %, then a paper sheet slides over it from the right.
3. **Diagram-to-3D** (V7 → F): the drawn postbiotic dots on the card scale up and dissolve into the glowing particles of the 3D animation.
4. **3D-to-diagram** (F → V8): the burst cloud fades into the yellow "good microbe" dots on the wall.
5. **Lower thirds** on D and F hide the burned-in English captions and keep everything in BM.

**Motion rules**
- Text animates on the word it's spoken. Highlights swipe in about 0.2 s after the word.
- Stamps (tags, badges) land with a quick overshoot and a soft "pop" SFX. Arrows draw on, never fade.
- A slow 3 % push-in on every scene. Nothing is ever fully static.

**Sound**
- A soft piano or pluck bed (or the music already on the footage), ducked about 8 dB under the VO.
- A paper-swipe SFX on Vox transitions, pops on stamps, and a low "whoomp" on the 3D burst at about 2:04.

## Things to check before production

1. **Packaging mismatch.** The footage shows a **pump bottle labelled "Eczema Relief Cream"**, but the poster and the 15 s ad show the **100 g "Skin Rebalance" tube**. Decide which pack is current. If it's the tube, keep shots D, E and H short and end on the tube in the CTA card, or regenerate those three shots with the tube.
2. **"Ask your pharmacist" vs. curalle.co.** The script ends with both. Drop whichever channel isn't live yet.
3. **The "1 in 5 children" statistic** is the commonly cited global upper range for childhood eczema. Confirm your source, or soften it to *"ramai kanak-kanak"*, before the ad runs.
4. **Claims wording.** The script says *membantu / redakan* (helps / soothes), not *rawat / sembuh* (treat / cure). That keeps it inside cosmetic-product advertising rules for a KKM-notified product, so keep it that way in edits.
5. **"Glycerosome technology"** comes from your footage. Make sure it matches what's actually in the Skin Rebalance formula before the VO says it.
6. **AI-generated footage label.** The clip has an "AI-generated videos" watermark. Meta and TikTok require the AI-content toggle when you upload.
